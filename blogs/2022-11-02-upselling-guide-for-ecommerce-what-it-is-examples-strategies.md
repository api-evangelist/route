Are you avoiding upselling because you’re afraid you’ll annoy your customers? Or maybe you’ve heard the term, but you don’t really understand what it is or how to do it. Done properly, upselling is just another way to make the customer journey more convenient, reduce the percentage of abandoned carts, and increase your sales revenue.
